About Warm Dry Home

Warm Dry Home is a small, independent site for people in the UK who want a warmer, drier home without a frightening energy bill. Damp, condensation and mould are a real problem in British homes, and the advice online is often written to sell the most expensive gadget.

We do it the other way round. For every guide we start with the numbers: how much power something uses, what that costs at current UK electricity prices, and whether there is a cheaper way to get the same result. Only after that do we suggest products.

How we choose products

  • We look at stated power use, real-world capacity and safety features rather than marketing claims.
  • We read owner reviews over several months, not just launch-week ratings.
  • We say clearly when a cheaper option or a free fix (like better ventilation) is enough.
