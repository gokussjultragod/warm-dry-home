The power on the label is the maximum. Appliances with a thermostat or humidistat often use much less once the room reaches the setting, so treat this as the worst case. Your price per kWh is on your energy bill or app.

How the sum works

Cost = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours × price per kWh. A 200 W dehumidifier running 8 hours uses 1.6 kWh. At 25p per kWh, that is 40p a day.
